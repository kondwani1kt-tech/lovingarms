Family Care Planning Checklist

Use this checklist to prepare for a care conversation. Do not include sensitive medical, financial or personal identification details on a public website form.

1. Daily routines
- What time does the person usually wake, eat and sleep?
- Which tasks are difficult: bathing, dressing, toileting, mobility, meals, housekeeping or errands?
- Are there fall risks or home safety concerns?

2. Preferences and dignity
- What matters most to the person about care at home?
- Are there cultural, language, privacy or routine preferences we should know?
- Which family members should be included in updates?

3. Schedule and coverage
- Is support needed occasionally, weekly, overnight or for short-term respite?
- What days and times are most important?
- What happens if a caregiver is unavailable?

4. Payment and authorization
- Are we discussing private pay, Medicaid questions, insurance or facility staffing?
- What eligibility or authorization questions should be confirmed?
- What terms should be reviewed before care begins?

5. Emergency planning
- Emergencies: call 911.
- Who is the family contact for urgent scheduling concerns?
- Which medications, allergies or mobility notes should be shared directly with qualified providers?

6. Questions to ask any provider
- Are you licensed or approved for the services you propose?
- How are caregivers screened, trained and supervised?
- What is the backup plan when a caregiver is absent?
- What are the rates, minimum hours and cancellation terms?
- How will care plans be reviewed and changed?
